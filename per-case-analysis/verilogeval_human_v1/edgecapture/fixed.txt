For each bit in a 32-bit vector, capture when the input signal changes from 1 in one clock cycle to 0 the next. out[i] becomes 1 on the clock edge after the 1-to-0 transition. "Capture" means that the output will remain 1 until the register is reset (active high synchronous reset).
