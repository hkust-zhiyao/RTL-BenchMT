
I would like you to implement a module named TopModule with the following
interface. All input and output ports are one bit unless otherwise
specified.

 - input  x (4 bits)
 - output f

The module should implement the function f shown in the Karnaugh map
below. The K-map labels x[1], x[2], x[3], x[4] correspond to the port
bits x[0], x[1], x[2], x[3] respectively (x[1] is the least-significant
bit of x and x[4] is the most-significant bit of x). d is don't-care,
which means you may choose to output whatever value is convenient.

                x[1]x[2]
  x[3]x[4]   00  01  11  10
        00 | d | 0 | d | d |
        01 | 0 | d | 1 | 0 |
        11 | 1 | 1 | d | d |
        10 | 1 | 1 | 0 | d |
